**How to resolve:** read Table 17 (nutritional status) and Figure 7 (exclusive breastfeeding) in the primary report, the [NDHS 2023–24 Key Indicators Report](https://dhsprogram.com/pubs/pdf/PR157/PR157.pdf) (National Population Commission, Federal Ministry of Health and ICF, September 2024). It also gives figures by zone (North West, North East, North Central), which this campaign should use.

## Health review

The health advice in the script (exclusive breastfeeding to six months, complementary foods from six months, early clinic visits for weight loss) follows standard WHO and UNICEF guidance. Have a nurse, doctor or nutrition officer at a local primary health care centre check the final wording before publishing.
