## Caveats

- The UNICEF page shows no publication date or survey year for these figures. Before publishing, confirm the year against UNICEF's Nigeria Education Fact Sheets (MICS data) and say "UNICEF estimates" rather than implying the figures are current.
- News reports quote other totals (often "10 million" or more, citing UBEC). Do not mix figures from different sources in one post.
- Prefer the female attendance figures for girl-child content: they are regional and directly relevant to the North.
