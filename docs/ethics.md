# Ethics and content rules

These rules apply to everything published from this project.

## Consent
- Only animate faces and voices of people who have given written consent, or of fully AI-generated presenters.
- Never depict real public figures, community leaders, victims or children who can be identified.

## Accuracy
- Every statistic needs a named source (UNICEF, UBEC, NBS, WHO, state ministries) and a date, recorded in the campaign's `sources.md`.
- No unverified claims about security incidents. Safety content points people to official channels only.

## Language and culture
- A native Hausa speaker reviews every Hausa script and caption before publishing. Machine-written Hausa is a draft, never final.
- Content respects local religious and cultural norms. Test drafts with a small group from the target community first.

## Transparency
- Label every post as AI-generated, using each platform's AI-content label as well as a line in the caption.
- Keep the generation settings (prompt, seed, model version) in the campaign folder so any output can be reproduced.

## Children
- Use illustrated or clearly AI-generated children only, in dignified, non-distressing scenes.
- Never show children in danger, distress or poverty for effect.
