# Ethics and content rules

These rules apply to everything published from this project.

## Accuracy
- Every factual claim needs a named, reliable source with a date, recorded in the topic's `sources.md`: peer-reviewed papers, reviews, WHO and national health or environment agencies, standard test methods, university or museum pages. Not blogs, social media or AI answers.
- A scientist who knows the field checks the English script before it is translated (`science_reviewed` in `script.json`).
- Say how sure the science is. Settled facts can be stated plainly; open questions are presented as open.
- Simplify, but never say something false to make it simpler. If a simplification leaves something out, the full version goes in `sources.md` notes.
- Correct mistakes in public: if a published post is wrong, post the correction.

## Health topics
- Explain the science; don't diagnose or prescribe. Point people to health workers, pharmacists and official guidance (WHO, NCDC, the state ministry of health).
- Never contradict official medical advice without saying so explicitly and citing why.

## Language and culture
- A native Hausa speaker writes or reviews every Hausa script and caption before publishing (`hausa_reviewed`). Machine-written Hausa is a draft, never final.
- Use the shared glossary (`glossary/terms.csv`) so a term means the same thing in every post. Prefer Hausa words people already use; when a new term is needed, give the English term beside it the first time.
- Science sometimes touches beliefs (origins, health, the natural world). Explain the evidence respectfully; don't mock beliefs or set science against faith.
- Test drafts with a small group of Hausa speakers who are not scientists: if they can't retell the idea, rewrite it.

## Consent
- Only animate faces and voices of people who have given written consent, or of fully AI-generated presenters.
- Never depict real public figures or identifiable children.

## Transparency
- Label every AI-generated image, voice or presenter, using each platform's AI-content label as well as a line in the caption.
- Keep the generation settings (prompt, seed, model version) in the topic folder so any output can be reproduced.
- Give sources: put the main one in the caption or a pinned comment.
