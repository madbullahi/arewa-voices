## Notes

- "Most colds get better in about a week" simplifies the 7–10 day figure; keep it that loose.
- Some sore throats and chest infections are bacterial: the script's last line (only when a health worker prescribes) covers this. Don't say "never take antibiotics for a cough".
- Look for a Nigerian source (NCDC or the Federal Ministry of Health national action plan on AMR) to add before publishing.
